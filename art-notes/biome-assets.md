# Ocean Feast — biome image asset brief

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool. PNGs are original, unedited generated outputs. Each atlas is a two-column, two-row layout; tiles are read in row-major order.

## Files

- `dist/assets/biomes-a.png`: coral lagoon; kelp forest; mangrove estuary; bluewater shelf.
- `dist/assets/biomes-b.png`: shipwreck; polar sea ice; mesopelagic canyon; abyssal hydrothermal vents.

Artistic natural-history environments, not photographs of actual locations or scientific reconstructions. Fine distant fish silhouettes appear in the shipwreck tile despite the no-prominent-fauna prompt. Each tile is independently usable and its center is kept largely open for gameplay.

## Prompt A

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: 2 by 2 background texture atlas for a realistic underwater browser game.
Create one single landscape PNG canvas, preferably 3072 × 1728 pixels (16:9 overall). Divide the canvas into EXACTLY two columns and two rows of equal size with NO gaps, NO borders and NO separator lines. Each of the four quadrants is its own independent 16:9 underwater photograph-like background. Camera is horizontally underwater looking across the water, never a surface landscape.
TOP LEFT: a tropical coral lagoon, clear luminous turquoise water, strong sunbeams from upper left, vibrant realistic staghorn corals and soft corals framing ONLY the lower edge and bottom corners.
TOP RIGHT: a Pacific giant kelp forest, deep jade and emerald water, long kelp fronds at far edges and in far background, golden shafts of filtered sun and layered atmospheric depth.
BOTTOM LEFT: submerged mangrove estuary, warm green water, intricate natural mangrove roots entering from upper corners down to bottom and sides, golden-green shafts of sunlight, sandy sediment floor.
BOTTOM RIGHT: a deep blue open-ocean continental shelf, immense cobalt and cyan negative space, distant dramatic reef wall only at bottom-right, subtle seafloor very low.
Within EACH quadrant preserve an unobstructed horizontally swimming area over the middle 65% of the tile. Each tile must stay independently coherent and full-bleed up to its cell edges. Photorealistic nature documentary quality, cinematic underwater light scattering, organic detail, fine natural particulate, believable depth, gently textured water rather than flat colour. All four tiles should be easy to distinguish. No prominent fish or other swimming animals, because separate sprites will be added. No labels, no typography, no writing, no UI, no title, no logos, no watermarks, no fantastical objects. Opaque background.
